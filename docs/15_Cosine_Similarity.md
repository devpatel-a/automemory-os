# Cosine Similarity

Cosine similarity measures how close two embeddings are.

It compares the direction of two vectors rather than their individual values.

Typical interpretation:

- 1.0 → Identical meaning
- 0.8 → Very similar
- 0.5 → Moderately related
- 0.0 → Unrelated

AutoMemory OS will use cosine similarity to retrieve memories based on semantic meaning rather than exact keywords.
